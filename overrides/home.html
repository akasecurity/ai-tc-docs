01 Detect Each event is scanned against your rule packs. Mostly regex, with a validator where shape is not enough: a Luhn checksum for card numbers, a Shannon-entropy check for secrets. 02 Decide Policy picks the outcome, per rule or per category. Prompts and tool inputs are checked before they reach the model; tool output and file reads after it responds. 03 Record Every match becomes a finding, kept on your machine. Rule id, category, severity and matched span, in one SQLite file: ~/.aka/data/aka.db.
